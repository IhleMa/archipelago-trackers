sts.json is generated from the Slay the Spire Archipelago world (https://github.com/cjmang/StS-AP-World,
worlds/spire): which part of each act every check belongs to, read from the world's regions, and its character list.
The icons in tracker.js are drawn for this tracker; no game art is used.
