data/sdv.json is generated from Archipelago's Stardew Valley world (worlds/stardew_valley in Archipelago 0.6.8, MIT licence,
by Kaito Kid, Jouramie and contributors): which kind of check each location is (from the tags in data/locations.csv), the
Community Center room of each bundle, the villagers with their birthday season, and the names of a few option values.
Which checks are in logic is decided by the world itself, rebuilt on the server from the slot's own options
(relay/ap_logic.py), not by this tracker.

data/pix.json holds pixel icons drawn for this tracker. No Stardew Valley artwork, sprites, logos or characters are used.
