data/oot.json is generated from Archipelago's Ocarina of Time world (worlds/oot in Archipelago 0.6.8, built on the
OoT Randomizer; MIT licence): the area and kind of every check. Which checks are in logic is decided by the world
itself, rebuilt on the server from the slot's own options (relay/ap_logic.py), not by this tracker. Seeds that shuffle
entrances, spawns or warp songs, or roll dungeon shortcuts or key rings at random, can't be rebuilt that way, and the
tracker says so.

data/icons.json holds icons drawn for this tracker. No Zelda artwork, logos, symbols or characters are used.
