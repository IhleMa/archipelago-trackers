data/wynncraft.json is generated from two sources:

1. The Wynncraft Archipelago world by EpicPuppy613 (https://github.com/EpicPuppy613/Wynncraft-APWorld,
   world version 0.5.0): territory levels and connections, zones and areas, every check with its level,
   territories, prerequisites and gear requirements, and the level and access rules. That repository has no
   licence file; only these facts are used, so the tracker can show the same rules the world uses.
   If the author asks, it will be removed.

2. Territory outlines (map coordinates) from Wynncraft's public territory list, as collected in
   https://github.com/jakematt123/Wynncraft-Territory-Info (territories.json). Only the coordinates are used.
