data/ac.json is generated from the Animal Crossing world in AP_ACGC_PC (https://github.com/randomouscrap98/AP_ACGC_PC,
apworld/animal_crossing, world version 0.5.5; the AP portion is MIT licensed): the museum's fossils, paintings, insects
and fish with the months and times they appear, the default fish/bug/fossil pass tables, the furniture and chess sets,
and the catalog category of each item. The logic follows the world's own rules (its feature files).

data/icons.json holds glyphs drawn for this tracker. No Animal Crossing artwork, logos or characters are used.
