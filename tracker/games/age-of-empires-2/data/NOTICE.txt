data/aoe.json and data/logic.json are generated from the Ageipelago world (worlds/age2de in https://github.com/dmwever/Archipelago,
MIT licence, by dmwever): the scenarios, their objectives and items, the buildings with their age and cost, and the world's
access rules. The rules are the world's own rule-builder rules after Archipelago resolves them, one rule graph per mix of
campaigns, and this tracker evaluates them the way Archipelago does. They were checked against real generation on 288 states.

data/icons.json holds icons drawn for this tracker. No Age of Empires artwork, logos or characters are used.
