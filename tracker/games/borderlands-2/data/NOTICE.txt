data/bl2.json is generated from the Borderlands 2 world in Bouncy Loot God (https://github.com/EdricY/Bouncy-Loot-God, worlds/borderlands2,
by EdricY): the areas of Pandora with their level ranges, fast-travel items and DLC, and the kind and area of every check.
Which checks are in logic is decided by the world itself, rebuilt on the server from the slot's own options
(relay/ap_logic.py downloads the world at the version the seed was made with), not by this tracker.

data/icons.json holds icons drawn for this tracker. No Borderlands artwork, logos or characters are used.
