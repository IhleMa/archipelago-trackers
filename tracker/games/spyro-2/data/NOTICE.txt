data/s2.json is generated from the Spyro 2 world in S2AP (https://github.com/Uroogla/S2AP, apworld/spyro2, by Uroogla, Garmo,
PhoenixAki and Sonofkrom): the homeworlds and their levels, and the kind of every check. Which checks are in logic is
decided by the world itself, rebuilt on the server from the slot's own options, key-locked levels and gemsanity picks
(relay/ap_logic.py downloads the world at the version the seed was made with), not by this tracker.

data/icons.json holds icons drawn for this tracker. No Spyro artwork, logos or characters are used.
