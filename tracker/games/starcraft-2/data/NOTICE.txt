data/sc2.json is generated from Archipelago's StarCraft II world (worlds/sc2 in Archipelago 0.6.8, MIT licence):
missions with their campaign and race, items with their race, type and the unit they upgrade, and the item groups
used to sort units by production building. Mission availability follows the SC2 client's own rules
(worlds/sc2/client.py, calc_available_nodes) using the mission order the world puts in slot data.

data/icons.json holds glyphs drawn for this tracker. No StarCraft artwork, logos or characters are used.
