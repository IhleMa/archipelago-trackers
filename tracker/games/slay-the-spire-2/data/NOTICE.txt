sts2.json is generated from the Slay the Spire 2 Archipelago world (https://github.com/dlueben1/Slay-the-Spire-2-Archipelago,
world/spire2): which part of each act every check belongs to, read from the world's regions.
The icons in tracker.js are drawn for this tracker; no game art is used.
